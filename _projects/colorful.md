Most social apps are built around photos, posts and comments. Colorful World asks what a
much quieter social network would look like: one where all you share is **how you feel,
as a color**.

- **Write a status, get a color.** Each status runs through an emotion lexicon (the NRC
  lexicon, via LeXmo), and its strongest emotion becomes a color: yellow for joy, blue for
  sadness, red for anger, and so on.
- **See your friends' colors** and how they've changed over time.
- **A live world map** shows the colors of everyone on Colorful World, placed with the
  browser's geolocation, so you can literally see how the world is feeling.

A five-person final project for a web development course, built with Flask and Tailwind.
