Shopping in a mall with friends is social: you wander together, ask "what do you think?",
and stumble onto things. Shopping online is a lonely list of search results and reviews from
strangers. **Jungle Mall** asks: what if the online marketplace were a place you could visit
together?

The Chrome extension turns Amazon.fr into a shared **virtual mall**. It reads what you're
looking at and places your avatar in the matching "department", so you can see your friends
moving through the store in real time.

- **Real-time presence:** friends appear as avatars in the department they're browsing.
- **Shared discovery:** click a friend to see what they're looking at, and go there together.
- **Quick reactions and proximity chat:** send "Deal!", "Recommend" or "Help?", or talk to
  whoever is nearby.

An Advanced UI course project at IP Paris, built with Soheil Lotfi and Keryan C. My parts
included the proximity chat and styling.
